# Stage 05 Assignment — Multi-Source Logistics Integration

Piedmont Logistics wants a single analytical view of fleet and shipment performance. The required information is spread across its operational database, CRM extract, fuel files, and GPS source.

Build an analytical dataset that supports:

- On-time delivery rate
- Delivery performance by warehouse
- Driver and vehicle utilization
- Fuel cost per shipment and per mile where supportable
- Customer geographic analysis
- Identification of source conflicts or unmatched records

Document:

- Identifier normalization rules
- Source-of-truth decisions
- Records excluded from the analysis
- Quality checks performed after integration

The business has not supplied a prebuilt crosswalk between every system.
