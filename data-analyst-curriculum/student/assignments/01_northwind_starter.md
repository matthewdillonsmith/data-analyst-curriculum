# Stage 01 Assignment — SQL and Excel Fundamentals

Complete the following using the clean starter dataset:

1. List all customers in North Carolina.
2. Count orders by state.
3. Calculate total line revenue for every order before discount.
4. Calculate net line revenue after discount.
5. Identify the ten customers with the highest net sales.
6. Summarize sales by product category.
7. Calculate monthly sales totals.
8. Identify products that have never been ordered.
9. Identify the employee responsible for the most sales.
10. Recreate at least three results in Excel using formulas or PivotTables.

For every query, state what one output row represents.
