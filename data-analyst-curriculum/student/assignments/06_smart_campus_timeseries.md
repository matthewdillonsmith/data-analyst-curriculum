# Stage 06 Assignment — Smart Campus Sensor Reliability and Energy Analysis

Facilities wants to understand building energy patterns but is unsure whether the sensor feed is reliable enough for management reporting.

Your job is to determine:

1. Whether the 15-minute data is complete enough for analysis.
2. How timestamp uniqueness should be defined.
3. Which readings appear technically invalid.
4. Which readings are unusual but could still be legitimate.
5. Typical energy profiles by building and hour.
6. Relationship between occupancy, HVAC state, and energy use.
7. How Daylight Saving Time affects interval counts and local-time reporting.
8. A recommended cleaned analytical representation of the time series.

Do not simply delete all statistical outliers. Explain each cleaning rule.
