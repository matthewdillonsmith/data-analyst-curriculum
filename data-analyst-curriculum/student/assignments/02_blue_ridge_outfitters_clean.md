# Stage 02 Assignment — Modern Retail Analysis

Blue Ridge Outfitters wants a management review of sales and fulfillment performance.

Produce an analysis that answers:

- Monthly revenue trend
- New versus repeat-customer activity
- Highest-revenue categories and products
- Average order value
- Cancellation rate
- Average order-to-ship and ship-to-delivery time
- Warehouse delivery performance
- Geographic revenue distribution

Use at least two tools among Excel, SQL, Python, and Power BI. Reconcile key totals between tools.
