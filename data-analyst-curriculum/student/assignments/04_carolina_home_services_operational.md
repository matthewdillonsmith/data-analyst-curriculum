# Stage 04 Assignment — Service Operations Performance

Leadership believes service completion times have become less predictable.

Determine:

- Typical work-order cycle time by service type
- How cycle time differs by priority
- Where in the workflow the most time is spent
- Open-work-order aging
- Technician workload and completion patterns
- Whether parts usage is associated with longer completion times
- Which records cannot be confidently included in SLA reporting and why

Your deliverable must include a documented rule for determining the completion timestamp of a work order.

Validate all joins before calculating financial or cycle-time totals.
