# Stage 07 Assignment — Enterprise Analytics Capstone

## Executive request

> Leadership wants a reliable view of active-member growth, revenue, payment behavior, service reliability, and operational workload. Different departments currently report different numbers. Determine why, build a defensible analytical view, and identify the most important business findings.

No table or field list is prescribed beyond the data dictionary.

Your project must include:

- Requirements and metric-definition document
- Source inventory and data lineage
- Data-quality assessment
- Reproducible SQL/Python transformation process
- Governed definition of an "active member"
- Revenue and payment analysis
- Service-order performance analysis
- Reliability/outage analysis
- At least one use of historical/effective-dated data
- Correct treatment of point-in-time snapshot data
- Dashboard or equivalent executive analytical product
- Technical documentation
- Final management presentation

Clearly separate facts, assumptions, unresolved questions, and recommendations.
